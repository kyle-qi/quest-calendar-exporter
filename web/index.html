uWaterloo Quest Calendar Exporter

Open-source replacement for the deprecated baraa.ca Chrome extension. Convert your Quest My Class Schedule page into a .ics file you can import into Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook.

Step 1 — Get your schedule HTML

  1. Sign in to Quest.
  2. Go to Academics → Enroll → My Class Schedule and pick the term.
  3. Switch to List View.
  4. Save the page (Ctrl/Cmd+S, "Webpage, complete" or "HTML only") or right-click and copy the rendered HTML.
